# TEASER:

Who is Arch Adams, and why is he wandering through the American Dream? In this episode, the narrator of Thinkopolis introduces himself—as the alter ego behind Thinkopolis. Trapped inside Chas Holloway’s head, and trapped inside the American Dream, he sends ideas to “the typist” who turns them into stories.

This episode introduces Arch Adams, who is the regular voice of Thinkopolis. Brandi Lane was introduced in the previous episode - so the Thinkopolis world now has an exotic jungle setting along with 2-point character opposition. 

# THE SCRIPT

The Thinkopolis Files
by Chas Holloway
(DRAFT: 1-5-2025),
241 words

ARCH ADAMS:

I should tell you who I am. The name’s Arch Adams. I’m that Thinkopolis writer’s alter ego. I’m the creative side of his brain.

Him, he’s a glorified typist.

Gets us a job on Substack doing a column called “Thinkopolis.” 
I know — stupid name. But the deal is, he’s gotta turn in stories about America. So me, the one who’s gotta walk around in his head, I get stuck here in the American dream.

I mean, actually lost in the Dream.  Which it turns out is a real place. Like I had nothing better to do. But they’re depositing checks … so, I write. 

He tells me we’ve got a big following.
I wouldn’t know.

Lemme tell you how we operate. I’m the one who thinks up the ideas. The typist — he couldn’t tie his shoes without me. I’m the key to his whole act. I send him idea. But here’s the deal. I tell him like it is. 

I tell him, the American indians — they had open borders, too.

I tell him Stanford students have more morality than southern Baptists.

I tell him, pet food bags should come with a toy inside.

And he writes it.

The thing I’ve learned about the American dream — you never know what savage thing you’re gonna meet next. When I find out, I’ll tell the typist, and we’ll let you know about it. 

Oh yeah, you’re in Thinkopolis — in case you didn’t know.

END
