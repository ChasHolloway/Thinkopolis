# Story Theory

Thinkopolis is built on a coherent theory of story construction. This section explains the principles used to create the Thinkopolis universe.

## Table of Contents

- - [Plot](02.%20Plot%20Construction.md)
- [Character](Character.md)
- [World Building](Setting.md)

## NOTE:

Thinkopolis begins as short-form, largely self-contained episodes. But those episodes are also building a persistent world—characters, locations, history, relationships, mysteries, and conflicts. As the body of episodes grows, those elements begin connecting into larger narrative structures. The principles in this section were originally developed for novels and screenplays, but they provide the structural foundation for that longer-form Thinkopolis story.
